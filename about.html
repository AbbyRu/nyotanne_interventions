About Nyota Nne

Supporting potential through understanding and collaboration.

Nyota Nne Interventions brings together specialist therapy, educational assessment and professional consultation.

Our Approach

Every individual deserves support that understands the whole person.

Our work is centred on understanding strengths, identifying barriers and developing practical strategies that support meaningful participation.

We believe effective support is collaborative. Families, educators, professionals and individuals all bring important knowledge to the process.

Our work is guided by

  • Individualised support
  • Evidence-informed practice
  • Inclusive education
  • Family collaboration
  • Multidisciplinary practice
  • Meaningful functional outcomes
