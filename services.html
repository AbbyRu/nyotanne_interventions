Our Services

Specialist support tailored to individual needs.

Our services are designed to support development, learning, participation, independence and inclusion.

OTEPEASEN
✦

Occupational Therapy

Support for participation in everyday activities, sensory processing, motor skills, independence and functional development.

May support:

  • Fine and gross motor skills
  • Sensory processing
  • Daily living skills
  • School participation
  • Functional independence
◈

Educational Psychology

Understanding how individuals learn and identifying strategies that support educational progress and wellbeing.

May include:

  • Exploring learning strengths and barriers
  • Consultation with families and educators
  • Recommendations for educational support
  • Planning for participation and progress
◎

Educational Assessments

Structured assessment to identify strengths, barriers, learning needs and appropriate recommendations.

Assessment may help:

  • Clarify patterns of strength and need
  • Inform educational planning
  • Guide practical support strategies
  • Support communication between home and school
◇

SEN Consultations

Collaborative guidance for families, schools and educators supporting learners with additional needs.

Consultation may cover:

  • Inclusive classroom strategies
  • Understanding learner needs
  • Support planning
  • Family and school collaboration
✓

Examination Access Arrangements

Support in understanding and exploring appropriate examination access arrangements where relevant.

Support may involve:

  • Reviewing learner needs
  • Considering appropriate arrangements
  • Providing relevant professional input
  • Supporting clear documentation
↔

Professional Consultation

Collaborative consultation and support for schools, professionals and organisations.

May include:

  • Case consultation
  • School and organisational guidance
  • Multidisciplinary collaboration
  • Referral and support planning
Finding the Right Service

Not every enquiry begins with a clear service name.

Start by telling us what you are noticing, what has already been tried and what you hope will change. We can help identify a suitable next step.

Discuss your needs

Helpful information to share

  • The main concern or barrier
  • Where the difficulty is most noticeable
  • Relevant school or professional information
  • Current strengths and successful strategies
  • The outcome you would like to work towards
