Who We Are

Educational & Occupational Therapy Services

We are a team of specialists providing Educational and Occupational Therapy Assessments and Interventions, and Special Educational Needs (SEN) Consultations. Our aim is to work alongside families and educational settings to enable children and young people to reach their full potential.

We believe in a holistic and child-centred approach. Each child and young person is unique and brings their own strengths and challenges. Our work is focused not just on addressing difficulties, but on building confidence and self-reliance.

Comprehensive, individualised support across Occupational Therapy, Education, and SEN.

Occupational Therapy Assessments

Comprehensive assessments of sensory, motor, and functional skills to guide targeted interventions. We evaluate fine motor, gross motor, sensory processing, and executive functioning in relation to school and daily life.

Fine Motor Gross Motor Sensory Executive Function

Occupational Therapy Interventions

Goal-oriented therapy designed to improve independence and participation in school and daily life. Interventions are practical, functional, and built around each child's individual goals.

Goal-Oriented Independence School Participation

The Specialists

Meet the People Behind the Work

Our team brings together expertise across Occupational Therapy, Special Educational Needs, and Educational Assessment — united by a shared commitment to every child's potential.

🩺

Occupational Therapist

OT Specialist

A qualified Occupational Therapist specialising in assessment and intervention for fine motor skills, gross motor development, sensory processing, and executive functioning. Interventions are practical, goal-oriented, and designed to improve independence and functional participation in school and daily life.

📖

Special Needs Teacher & Specialist Assessor

SEN Specialist

An experienced Special Needs Teacher and Specialist Assessor with expertise in identifying learning differences and educational barriers. Provides comprehensive educational assessments, targeted intervention programmes, and detailed reports to guide effective support strategies.

🏫

SENDCo — SEND Coordinator

Inclusion Specialist

Providing strategic oversight and guidance for inclusive practice within schools. Supporting the development of Individual Education Plans (IEPs), coordinating interventions, and working closely with staff and families to ensure appropriate provision and progress monitoring.

📋

Access Arrangements Specialist

Assessments Expert

Experienced in assessing and recommending appropriate examination access arrangements. Providing guidance on reasonable adjustments in line with regulatory requirements, ensuring all learners have equitable access to assessments and examinations.
